# Verified research sources (accessed November 14, 2026)

1. NLBSE 2027 organizers. **Tool Competitions — Multitask Code Classification.** https://nlbse2027.github.io/tools/ . Official definitions, required model, publication rules, competition metric and example labels.
2. NLBSE 2027 organizers. **Code-comment-classification repository.** https://github.com/nlbse2027/code-comment-classification . Dataset schema and split sizes; baseline results; preprocessing; model architecture.
3. Mock, M., Melegati, J., Kretschmann, M., Diaz Ferreyra, N. E., and Russo, B. (2024). **MADE-WIC: Multiple Annotated Datasets for Exploring Weaknesses In Code.** *ASE 2024*, pp. 2346–2349. https://doi.org/10.1145/3691620.3695348 . Dataset provenance.
4. Russo, B., Melegati, J., and Mock, M. (2025). **Leveraging Multi-Task Learning to Improve the Detection of SATD and Vulnerability.** *ICPC 2025*, pp. 1–12. https://doi.org/10.1109/ICPC66645.2025.00017 . Directly related multitask modeling research.
5. Feng, Z., et al. (2020). **CodeBERT: A Pre-Trained Model for Programming and Natural Languages.** *Findings of EMNLP 2020*, pp. 1536–1547. https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2020.findings-emnlp.139 . Encoder used in baseline.

The first two are competition documentation, not peer-reviewed research papers. References 3–5 have DOI identifiers supplied by official NLBSE sources; verify publication styling against the submission template before submission.
